# 1. Scope

## Building

1. **Input:** a form where marketers enter the content of a social.
2. **Set-up:** where the designer defines templates and the rules for what each one allows (by ticking boxes).

## Not building (for now)

- **Artwork and rendering.** The social creative app does this.
- **Workflow:** approvals, statuses, review steps. This comes later. The first goal is getting the right information into the right place.

## Fixed facts

- **Canvas:** portrait, one size for everything. The exact size is still open; see [06](06-open-questions.md).
- **Timing:** every slide (page) is on screen for **10 seconds**.
- **Outro:** every social ends with one, on screen for **3 seconds**. Marketers have **no** fields on it; it's added automatically.
- **Backgrounds:** about **7**, each with a PNG overlay. The designer calls these "templates".
- **Layouts:** about **5** slide layouts. More will be added.
- **Shared elements** on every slide:
  - **Pill** (top right): a text label on a pill shape.
  - **CTA** (bottom left).
  - **Page dots** (bottom right): automatic.

## Why

Today marketers fill in everything, often get it wrong, and the designer fixes it when making the social. Once the input is correct, the socials can be made instantly.
